ci(pr-automation): resolve the reviewer plan once

The two reviewer jobs decided a delayed retry with the same thirty-line
step script, three jobs each reinterpreted the caller's required-reviewer
policy, and every reviewer shipped its result and its stage report as two
separate artifacts.

The retry decision now lives in review-retry.js and the jobs differ only
in the stage they log. The evidence job settles the mandatory reviewer set
before any evidence work and publishes it, so later stages read that plan
instead of deriving it again; a stage that cannot read it treats every
selected reviewer as mandatory. Reviewer results travel in one artifact
per reviewer, and the report reads provider token usage in the runtime's
own spelling once rather than guessing among four.
